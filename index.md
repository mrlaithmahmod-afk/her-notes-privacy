# Privacy Policy for Her Notes

**Last updated:** January 2026

Her Notes respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and protected.

## Information We Collect
Her Notes collects information you provide directly, including:
- Account information (such as name and email when signing in)
- Notes, journal entries, affirmations, and related content
- App usage data to improve functionality

## How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Provide and improve app features
- Sync your data securely across devices
- Generate insights based on your entries
- Maintain account security

## Data Storage and Security
Your data is stored securely using industry-standard services. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

## Subscriptions
Her Notes offers optional paid subscriptions. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not store your payment information.

## Data Deletion
You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting:
support@hernotes.app

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

## Contact
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact:
support@hernotes.app
